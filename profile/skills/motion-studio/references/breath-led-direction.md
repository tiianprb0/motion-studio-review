# Research and meaning-led visual development

Read ../../../CREATIVE-BRIEF.md. Research every new script/topic before deciding visuals; record sources and distinguish verified facts from interpretations. Recognizable real places require specific researched details. Optional source callouts must suit context.

Map narrative meaning units to concurrent visual explanations. A breath may change composition, camera emphasis, actor action or information without changing location. Split long multi-idea sentences into meaning beats. Do not use a fixed number of shots or compulsory 2.5–4/10–15 second schedules for all scripts.

Master world/scene persists while camera focus changes. Composition is rich in environmental detail, with clear main focus. Metaphors must show the actual meaning: a turning clock alone means elapsed time, not repeated behavior. Repeated events require fresh visual staging rather than replay of the same scene.

Narrative follows the topic; Naya/Yan remain recurring characters without occupying every explanatory beat. Extra contextual information and humor are allowed. Show the narrated objects/events at the relevant time, and use camera emphasis aligned with emotional/narrative emphasis. Greyscale direction signals memory/flashback.
