# Remotion Shotcraft for Hermes
Read ../motion-studio/SKILL.md and its standing brief. This is an adaptation of Video Shotcraft to narrated human stories, not an instruction to turn them into product promos.

## Library
Read [source provenance](SOURCE.json). The complete pinned upstream is at assets/vendor/video-shotcraft/. Start with references/shots/ card metadata; read selected cards fully and follow each explicit demo path. Never guess a demo filename from a card name. Read demos/README.md for shared imports before copying components. Gallery names and variants should be resolved against the pinned files, not remembered counts.

Before raw-script work, read ../motion-studio/references/storyboard-first.md and approved-v18.md in the same folder. Storyboard is an internal first step; do not wait for user ACC. New scripts requested for production authorize needed configured-voice narration without another approval question. V18 is the current approved execution baseline, retaining V12 identity foundations. Upstream approval gates do not override this standing authorization.

## Story before effect
Map each emotional/story beat to a primary action and a reason for motion. Select cards by intent and constraints: reveal new information, transfer attention, bridge time/place, contrast a viewpoint or accent a realization. Record chosen card/variant, exact source, what changes, and why it helps. Prefer a small coherent vocabulary over demonstrating the library. Create original staging when no card fits; humans and acting remain first-class subjects.
Use wide/medium/close/overhead variation when motivated. Camera movement should reveal context or a reaction, not merely enlarge the same illustration. Preserve spatial continuity and a focal point across cuts. Combine anticipation, accent, follow-through and secondary action; avoid layers competing at the same instant.
Read [vertical and motion adaptation](references/adaptation.md) before porting a demo. Use upstream pipeline.md and aesthetic-rules.md selectively as technical references. Their product-specific template, music-first pacing, mandatory durations, landscape defaults and approval gates do not override the user's brief or current authorization.

For authorized production, make a short moving proof for difficult motion before full export, then use ../remotion-motion-review/SKILL.md. Current installation alone does not authorize those renders.
