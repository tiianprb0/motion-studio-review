# Adapt recipes to less.certain
## Layout and camera
Most upstream demos target 1920x1080. Recompose at 1080x1920 rather than stretching or center-cropping. Derive subject bounds in world space and inspect transformed bounds under camera motion. Reserve screen-space title, logo and caption zones; overlays must not inherit the scene camera. Track face/hand/prop paths throughout entrances and transitions. Explicit intended contact is distinct from accidental collision.
Preserve expressive human poses, gaze, changing emotion and believable support/contact with seats, floors and objects. Abstract UI cards can inform choreography without replacing people. A page camera demo is a technique reference, not the required scene model.
## Motion parameters
Choose a consistent family of easing, acceleration, stagger and settling suited to heboh tapi elegan. Treat upstream durations, overshoot and hold values as starting points, not universal requirements. Allocate energy to narrative accents and quieter beats to comprehension. Sustained engagement can come from reaction or reframing; every frame need not move.
Use frame-driven deterministic Remotion animation, clamped interpolation and seeded randomness where needed. Audit nested Sequence frame origins, transitions/overlaps and audio lead before copying timing. Avoid Date.now, unseeded Math.random and time-dependent CSS animation for rendered motion.
## Typography and caption timing
Titles are concise and integrated with action. Kinetic title motion must not move the caption track or obscure faces. Captions use natural Indonesian meaning units even for English speech; timing follows phrase meaning, not forced word correspondence. Maintain reading time and balanced line breaks. Test actual font loading and real glyph bounds. Apply approved V18 captions (../../motion-studio/references/approved-v18.md): uniform44px, per-word backgrounds, nine anchors and column-based alignment, short entrance then stable hold, screen-space platform-safe reading envelope. Slide transitions and occasional subtitle-focused camera cuts are explicitly allowed when purposeful.
