## Approved visual contract
Simple expressive humans/objects, rich fast purposeful motion—heboh tapi elegan. Current duo Naya/Yan follows V12 face/wardrobe reference. No numbered episodes, “cerita kecil” or presentation headers. Selective titles, slide transitions and occasional subtitle-focused camera cuts are allowed where meaningful.

Natural Indonesian captions, uniform44px at1080×1920, per-word backgrounds. Nine positions; alignment follows column (left/center/right), never an emphasis mode. Animate in briefly, then hold stable. Keep text independent of scene camera and inside the project reading envelope x90..900,y280..1240 including entry, away from faces/props and platform UI. Side phrases over half frame use two semantic rows, max540px/line, retaining44px; choose contrasting word tiles. Character dialogue uses quotation marks near the speaker, without bubble backgrounds. See approved-v18.md for details and inherited anchors.

Outro has a purposeful transition, elegant less.certain wordmark, and ONE horizontal row [TikTok SVG][Instagram SVG] @less.certain. Use local SVG paths, not duplicate usernames. Garment logo follows the torso; hands/props have convincing contact.
