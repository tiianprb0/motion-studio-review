# Narration-first audio
Use this reference when the task includes audio design. Resolve the active recording/timing first. A visual-only revision changes neither narration nor audio mix without an additional user request. Do not call ElevenLabs to regenerate unchanged narration.
Read ../../remotion-shotcraft/assets/vendor/video-shotcraft/references/sound-design.md and sfx-sourcing.md for sound work; music-beat-sync.md only when music is relevant. Adapt the music-first promo approach: less.certain speech and emotional phrasing anchor the edit, with musical accents fitted around them. Never accelerate existing speech or retime its captions merely to hit beats.
For candidate-only searches provide source/preview links without downloading or changing the project. When sourcing is authorized, preserve per-asset source, license and required attribution. Assess candidates in context; metadata or waveform analysis does not prove audition. Do not imply the five bundled BGM tracks are automatically cleared for every use.
Keep separate local narration, BGM and SFX tracks and a frame-based cue table tied to the shot timeline. Use measured levels, listen for intelligibility, duck music under speech, and inspect clipping after mixing. Avoid adding impacts to every beat. Final renders read fixed local assets, not live searches. Reuse existing recording cache; OAuth tokens are never copied into notes.

## Standing new-script authorization
A new script requested for video production authorizes needed narration directly in the configured/chosen voice, without an extra credit or permission question. Estimate/log usage as production information, cache jobs/results, and never duplicate generation while an outcome is unresolved. Storyboard is prepared internally first and does not require ACC. Visual-only revisions reuse the actual existing recording/timing; requested spoken edits reuse unchanged portions where practical. A documentation-only request does not generate audio. See storyboard-first.md for workflow scope.
