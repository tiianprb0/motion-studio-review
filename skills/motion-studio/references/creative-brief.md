## Current approved direction
V12 is the visual baseline: simple humans/objects with rich, purposeful, fast motion—heboh tapi elegan. Keep people expressive and interactions convincing. ## Captions and branding
Natural Indonesian captions, translated by meaning and timed by phrase. Same44px base size at1080×1920 wherever placed. Per-WORD backgrounds; phrases enter together, then remain stable. Nine anchors: left/center/right × top/middle/bottom, with left/center/right alignment matching the COLUMN. Keep overlays independent of camera. Choose locations around subjects and platform UI, inside the conservative reading envelope x90..900,y280..1240 including entry motion; do not guarantee against all expanded platform overlays. No accidental face/prop/logo collisions or clipping.

Brand less.certain. Retain upper-right watermark with clear space appropriate to the layout. Outro: animated wordmark and one row [TikTok SVG][Instagram SVG] @less.certain. One shared username, purposeful transition into the outro. SVG icons are supported in Remotion. No rejected traveling-dot default.

## Production and audio authorization
Storyboard remains a required internal creative step BEFORE implementation. Write audience assessment and shot plan with actions, emotion, focus, framing, camera and transitions. Continue directly to production without asking for storyboard ACC. A new script given for production authorizes needed audio generation in the configured voice without a separate credit/permission question. Estimate/log usage and cache results; do not duplicate generations. A documentation-only or planning-only request remains limited to that scope.

Visual revisions MUST reuse actual existing audio and timing. Same voice with resynthesis is not reuse. For requested spoken edits preserve unchanged passages and generate only the needed change where feasible. Resolve actual active source/audio from Root.tsx; V12 currently uses narration-v7.mp3 and timing-v7.json. Use connected OAuth MCP and preserve credentials.
