# Crowd and held-prop continuity

User correction during V19 production,1 November2026: supporting demonstrators must not duplicate Naya/Yan. Preserve the established main characters when participating, but create distinct supporting faces, hair/headwear, clothing and proportions, with a separate layer of silhouettes for depth. Animate purposeful group movement/weight shifts or placard raising; do not clone the leads or substitute a static crowd. Keep silhouettes simple and readable, and avoid endless synchronized reset loops.

Every carried placard must visibly connect to a hand. Define a grip anchor shared by actor pose, pole and palm; put them in the same transform hierarchy or derive them from the same local coordinates. Translate/rotate/lift the carrier and board together. Paint fingers/palm over the pole to show grip. Test contact through entrance, motion and exit, not just one still. Keep poles and sign boards clear of faces/captions and inside intended framing. Do not fix floating boards merely by making a longer pole near an unrelated hand.

For V19 specifically, demonstration scenes use stylized Bundaran HI Jakarta: Selamat Datang monument, circular fountain, surrounding skyline. Visual reference consulted: https://indonesiakaya.com/pustaka-indonesia/patung-patung-jakarta/ . Preserve less.certain palette. This location is story-specific, not a standing setting for every film. New crowds/landmarks and the V19 export remain candidates pending user review; V18 remains approved style. Existing V19 recording/timing must be reused on these revisions.
