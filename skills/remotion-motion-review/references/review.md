# Evidence-based review
## Coverage
Read the current audience notes and shot plan. Check each shot at entrance, anticipation, accent, mid-action, transition, and final hold; sample additional frames around high-risk movement. Inspect a moving proof for timing, camera continuity, secondary action, acting, and sustained development. Stills alone cannot establish motion quality. Record exactly which artifacts were inspected.
## Creative checks
Does each beat progress after entrance? Does the camera reveal information? Does energy have hierarchy and contrast? Do people show readable gaze, pose and emotion appropriate to the narrative? Is accountability or change shown as behavior? Are props grounded and hands attached convincingly? Are repeated layouts, generic metaphors or effect-only transitions weakening the story?
## Layout and text
Inspect faces, hands, phones, bubbles, title, logo and caption across camera transforms. Distinguish intentional contact/crop from accidents. Audit projected geometry when useful but verify actual rendered frames; a V5-only audit is not valid for V6 without adaptation. Captions use the approved nine-anchor grid: column-left aligns left, column-center centers, column-right aligns right. Uniform44px at1080×1920, per-word backgrounds, natural Indonesian meaning units and reading time. Check the full word rectangles and entrance path inside x90..900,y280..1240, plus faces/props/UI. Check English narration is translated in the rendered caption source, not merely declared Indonesian in metadata.
## Technical delivery when rendering is authorized
Verify composition ID, actual final file, dimensions, fps, duration, audio streams, sync and complete decode. Derive excerpts from the final export. Verify output/source freshness and version-specific paths. A successful decode does not establish creative quality.
## Findings record
For each finding record shot, time/frame, observed issue, impact, proposed fix and inspected evidence. Keep technical pass/fail separate from creative assessment and from user approval. Save findings only in authorized destinations, preserve earlier reports, and explicitly state if moving footage/audio was not inspected.
